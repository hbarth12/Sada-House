House

An Intellectual Hub in Damascus.

We bring people together around book talks, panels, film screenings, and even the occasional trivia night.

Sada House is a shared home in Mazra’a District, Damascus, founded by Syria Direct and Syria Civic.

Here, people across professions and generations can meet, exchange ideas, and find collaborators.

Our lending library offers books, newspapers, and reference materials to anyone in the city.
